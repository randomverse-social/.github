An algorithm-free social platform built for serendipitous content discovery, where every swipe is a surprise.

# 🎲 Social, without expectations.

Randomverse is built around a simple idea:

> «You shouldn't need to build an audience to be discovered.»

Instead of following people, chasing popularity, or scrolling through a personalized feed, Randomverse lets you discover unexpected photos, videos, and text from people around the world.

Every swipe can take you somewhere different.

- No follower-based feed
- No popularity contest
- Just content waiting to be discovered

---

# 🌍 Discover the unexpected

Randomverse is designed for serendipity.

You might see something funny from one part of the world, followed by a photograph from somewhere you've never been, followed by a completely unexpected thought from someone else.

The point isn't to predict exactly what you want to see.

> «The point is to help you find things you weren't looking for.»
